How TRAP-FL Works

1
Input Traffic

Network packet features are extracted from 42 attributes of the UNSW-NB15 dataset.

2
IDS Detection

The trained MLP classifies traffic as Normal or Malicious with a probability confidence score.

3
Risk Scoring

Temporal risk is computed via EMA of confidence scores to detect sustained malicious behavior.

4
IPS Response

The IPS engine triggers ALLOW, MONITOR, ALERT, or BLOCK based on combined risk and probability.
